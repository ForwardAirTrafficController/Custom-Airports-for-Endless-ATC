# Istanbul TMA – Endless ATC Custom Airport File

## Overview
- **Name:** Yesilkoy APP/Radar
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Last updated:** 27.07.2026
- **Difficulty:** Hard 
- **Recommended for:** Complex TMA operations with point merge system. 

## Airspace Area
- **Location:** Northwestern Türkiye, Black Sea. 
- **Size:** 72.000 Km² (Including Lower ANK ACC Sectors 1, 2, 3 and 4)
- **Key airports included:**
  - `LTFM` Istanbul Airport - Biggest and busiest airport of Europe including 6 runways. Sequencing is becomes very challenging at peak hours. Point Merge System reduces delays and helps with sequencing.  - . 
  - `LTBA`  – Atatürk Airport - It was the main airport of Istanbul back then but replaced by new Istanbul airport. It had 3 runways but two of them decommissioned. Atatürk airport currently serves mainly business jets and aircraft coming for maintenance facilities. 
  - `LTFJ` - Sabiha Gökçen Airport - Sabiha Gökçen is placed at Asian side of Istanbul and mainly serves Regional and International traffic of Asian side. Airport is hub of "Pegasus Airlines". Airport has 2 parallel runways allowing independent operations. Generally one runway is used as landings and other as takeoffs. Also features Point Merge System.  
 
    
## Airspace Structure

Istanbul TMA is a very complex one. LTFM will have capability of 4 simultaneous departures at the same time with new runway 09 which is currently included at the file with official SIDs.  

For the STARs, LTFM has up to 4 different STAR for same beacon. All of these are implemented and all STARs for LTFJ and LTBA implemented also all Fixs are present for the use. 

## Notes & Known Limitations

Realistic air traffic (airlines) not added yet. P-D-R areas not implemented, TMA sectors are not implemented.
